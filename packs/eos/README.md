# EOS terminology pack for Motion (community, unofficial)

Renames Motion's concepts to the terms used by companies running on EOS (Rocks, Level 10, V/TO, IDS…).

EOS®, Traction®, Level 10 Meeting™, V/TO™ and related terms are trademarks of EOS Worldwide, LLC.
This pack is not affiliated with or endorsed by EOS Worldwide.

Install: Settings → Terminology → Import pack → paste `@motion-community/pack-eos`, upload `pack.json`, or paste its URL.
